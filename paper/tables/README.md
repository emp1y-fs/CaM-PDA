# Final-paper table data

These CSV files transcribe the displayed values and headers from the final author proof. They preserve the paper’s rounding, units and multi-level headers; they are not a fresh benchmark run. Use the captions and source-data links below when interpreting columns. The continued part of Table A4 is a separate CSV.

| Table | Page | CSV | Caption |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 1 | 10 | [table_1.csv](table_1.csv) | NYUv2 test-set AbsRel on 654 images. Registered baseline settings retain each model's modality and internal processing; lower values indicate smaller error. |
| 2 | 10 | [table_2.csv](table_2.csv) | DREDS-CatNovel results on 11,520 targets. AbsRel is reported for the whole image, input holes, annotated difficult materials and depth boundaries. Material support is available on 11,508 targets. Whole-image MAE and RMSE are in millimeters; bold marks the lowest reported value in each column. |
| 3 | 10 | [table_3.csv](table_3.csv) | ICL-NUIM results on 80 targets. Whole-image MAE and RMSE are in meters; remaining entries are AbsRel. |
| 4 | 11 | [table_4.csv](table_4.csv) | MoE architecture versus a functional equal-capacity dense control under shared balanced-confidence conditions. AbsRel and sample SD across three paired seeds are multiplied by 10^3. Group-macro reductions and paired-group 95% intervals measure this component contrast. |
| 5 | 12 | [table_5.csv](table_5.csv) | Regional AbsRel for one frozen paired retraining of the final three-expert architecture and an independently trained dense residual control, each with 98,076,526 completion parameters. DREDS and STD diagnostic pools contain 180 and 40 frames; region-specific counts exclude unsupported regions. Positive reductions favor MoE. The observed complement excludes difficult materials, sensor holes and boundaries. Values are frame-macro means from one paired training seed. Full-split three-seed results appear in Table [tab:completed518-three-seed]. |
| 6 | 12 | [table_6.csv](table_6.csv) | Independent ablations of the final expert architecture. Values are relative whole-image AbsRel reductions against full MoE, in percent. Removing E, N or R deletes the corresponding residual, router and gain. Fixed routing computes all residuals without learned router selection. |
| 7 | 12 | [table_7.csv](table_7.csv) | Three paired training seeds for the final CaM-PDA and the no-expert shared-confidence PDA. Errors are image-macro AbsRel averaged across seeds. Reduction is calculated within each pair, then reported as mean ± sample standard deviation across the three pairs; positive values favor CaM-PDA. This control is separate from the equal-capacity dense control. |
| 8 | 13 | [table_8.csv](table_8.csv) | Deployment-pair DREDS expert-refinement effects on 11,508 material-eligible images. Specular and transparent pixels form the difficult-material region; its complement contains diffuse objects and background. Both columns use the same input screening and common-parameter training exposure. |
| 9 | 13 | [table_9.csv](table_9.csv) | Paired regional AbsRel reductions for CaM-PDA. Native PDA measures the complete-system contrast; shared-confidence PDA measures the expert-training increment and is not parameter-matched. Intervals use 5000 paired resamples of 45 filename groups, conditional on fixed checkpoints. They do not quantify training-seed variability. Positive reductions favor CaM-PDA; unsupported material regions are excluded. |
| 10 | 15 | [table_10.csv](table_10.csv) | Rank and insertion-position sensitivity using paired training and 108 validation inputs. All configurations use the same native-518 recipe and fixed geometry endpoint. Block indices are zero-based; positive percentages indicate larger validation error. |
| 11 | 16 | [table_11.csv](table_11.csv) | Anchor-screening comparison with the same frozen official PDA on 84 validation images. Full, hole and anomaly regions have 84, 46 and 60 eligible images. The anomaly proxy includes holes and sensor errors above (10 mm,2%D^gt). MAE/RMSE are in meters. |
| 12 | 16 | [table_12.csv](table_12.csv) | Standardized warm input-to-depth inference on a common 392×392 observation/output grid. Model-specific internal resolutions, modalities and native settings are retained. Parameters count resident learned components, including required priors. Allocated memory is the maximum Torch GPU allocation; CPU IP-Basic has no such allocation. |
| 13 | 19 | [table_13.csv](table_13.csv) | Bidirectional mean surface distance in millimeters. Regions are defined from reference span and chord geometry; the upper region overlaps the pose-fitting band. |
| 14 | 19 | [table_14.csv](table_14.csv) | Same-capture blue-light comparison in millimeters. P denotes prediction and R the fixed 137,383-point visible-side reference. The first three distances use each method' s finite positive ROI points; the last uses identical 19,331 raw-valid pixel locations for all methods. Symmetric mean is the equal-weight mean of the two directional means. No-expert PDA shares CaM-PDA' s confidence and depth-adaptation protocol but has no expert branches. |
| 15 | 20 | [table_15.csv](table_15.csv) | Processing sensitivity of the final blade cloud pair. Values are symmetric mean C2C distances in millimeters. Fixed support retains every original point after pose refitting; processed support also reflects point retention. |
| A1 | 25 | [table_a1.csv](table_a1.csv) | Screening-policy integration on a frozen development checkpoint. Counts pool evaluable observations from 84 images; AbsRel is frame-averaged over 84 full images and 46 eligible hole regions. Count-trained and depth-trained denote learned screening objectives. Higher \(J\) and lower AbsRel are preferred. |
| A2 | 27 | [table_a2.csv](table_a2.csv) | Whole-image AbsRel reductions against original no-expert shared-confidence PDA (%). Group intervals condition on the three trained pairs. |
| A3 | 27 | [table_a3.csv](table_a3.csv) | Regional AbsRel reductions against no-expert shared-confidence PDA (%). Material means annotated specular/transparent support where available; the procedural label is the supplied reflective support. Values below zero denote larger error. |
| A4 | 28 | [table_a4.csv](table_a4.csv) | All 24 sensitivity settings. Changes compare whole-image AbsRel with nominal; positive means higher error. Hole and boundary columns retain their eligible-image support. |
| A4 | 28 | [table_a4_continued.csv](table_a4_continued.csv) | Cont. |

| A6 | 29 | [table_a6.csv](table_a6.csv) | Local AbsRel reductions(%). The last column compares the full model with its same-role residual-zero counterfactual. Neither gate activation nor intended role guarantees lower local error. |
| A7 | 30 | [table_a7.csv](table_a7.csv) | Resource measurements. Times are seconds; reserved GPU and process RSS are GiB. Operation estimates exclude unsupported operations and are not comparable total FLOPs. |
| A8 | 31 | [table_a8.csv](table_a8.csv) | Additional native-method metrics for STD, NYUv2 and the procedural diagnostic. DREDS and ICL-NUIM are reported in Tables [tab:dreds-complete] and [tab:icl-native]; all values use complete registered coverage. |
